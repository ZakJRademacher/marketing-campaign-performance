# Marketing follow-up investigation

**For:** marketing operations manager. **Decision:** which contact-history population merits an experiment.

The full source contains 41,188 observations, 4,640 subscriptions (11.3%) and 105,754 recorded current-campaign contacts. Previous-success observations show 894 subscriptions among 1,373 records (65.1%). This population is highly selected; its rate is not incremental campaign lift.

Records ending with six or more contacts account for 32,350 recorded contacts and 186 subscriptions among 3,385 observations (5.5%). Records ending with one contact show 13.0%. These are final contact counts: unsuccessful records may receive more attempts. The difference does not establish that stopping earlier improves outcomes.

**Proposed next step:** compare previous outcomes within the same channel and observed month, flag small cells, then scope a randomized follow-up-protocol trial in an adequately sized population. Evaluate subscription rate and contacts per observation together. Record staff time and campaign costs prospectively if an economic evaluation is needed.

**Limits:** month labels pool years; no fabricated monthly chronology. The full source is documented as May 2008-November 2010, but this CSV has no explicit year column. Call duration is excluded from actionable pre-contact analysis. No customer identities, individual targeting rules, costs, ROI or causal contact-frequency effects are inferred.
