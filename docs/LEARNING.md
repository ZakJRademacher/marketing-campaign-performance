3. Compare May and cellular in SQL: 5,518 records, 614 subscriptions and 12,488 contacts. Calculate 614/5518 and reconcile the dashboard.
4. Use the same selections on the investigation page. Explain why six-plus final contacts do not prove that making extra calls reduces conversion.
