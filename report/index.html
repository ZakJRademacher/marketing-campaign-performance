03THE NEXT CHECK

Test one standardized follow-up, prospectively.

The gap holds within every observed month and channel, which makes it worth investigating. A randomized trial that offers one extra follow-up after an unsuccessful first contact, measured over 30 days with a workload guardrail, would show whether follow-up changes outcomes.

A proposed experiment.
No trial has run, and no lift is claimed.
